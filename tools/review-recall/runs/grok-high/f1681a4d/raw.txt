The poll-path refresh matches how `Timeout.asyncWait` (zio.select) arms duration timers: `loopAdd` → `armTimer` from the cached snapshot, after `poll` returns. Default `defer_callback` defers I/O callbacks until `processCompletions`, which now runs after that refresh. The regression test arms via `Loop.add` after an I/O-woken wait, which is the case the trailing `checkTimers` used to miss.
