file: examples/cq_spurious_select_repro.zig lines 91-132
title: Spurious-win return (and other errors) skip CQ teardown, leaving stack ops in the loop.
The file header and the generation-success path tear down with close(); cancelAll(.keep); drain, which is the documented single-driver contract. The path this reproducer exists to take does not: on error.Closed it prints SPURIOUS and `return error.Spurious`, and every `try` in `driver` (select, submit, yield) does the same. recv_op, t1, and cq live on the coroutine stack; cancel never runs, then the defers close the socketpair under an in-flight NetRecv. join() then returns that stack to the pool while the other seven workers keep running. ownerCallback will lock a freed CompletionQueue and push a freed group node; io_uring/kqueue may complete the recv on a recycled fd. The CQ tests themselves use `defer cq.cancelAll(.discard)` for exactly this. Put that defer next to `init()` (ops are frame-local, so .discard is the right Results), and only then return.
