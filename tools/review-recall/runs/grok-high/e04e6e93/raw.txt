The commit splits tick ownership so `poll` refreshes the awake snapshot before `checkTimers` and again after the backend wait. That matches the stated bugs (stale poll timeout vs. backdated `Loop.add` after a sleep). Two issues remain: the new pre-poll path is untested, and the new “owner-thread only” claim on `updateNow` does not match `setTimer`.
