title: The teardown defer closes the socketpair descriptors while still holding feed_mu, landing the deadlocking version the commit message says was replaced.
The inner `defer sh.feed_mu.unlock()` runs when the outer defer block exits, i.e. AFTER both `std.c.close` calls, so the closes execute under the mutex hold. The commit message states the opposite contract ('the driver clears the descriptor under the mutex BEFORE closing it after unlock', 'The close must stay OUTSIDE the mutex') and reports that closing under the hold deadlocked about one run in ten on macOS: close blocks in the kernel on the driver's executor thread, the flooder parks on feed_mu forever, and the remaining executor sleeps in kevent with no wake coming.
